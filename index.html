The Process

Not a waffle.
A commitment.

Every LIÈGUER waffle begins 72 hours before it reaches you. The brioche dough — enriched with European butter, slow-developed with time — is cold-fermented to develop a caramelized crust that shatters on contact.

Pearl sugar imported from Belgium collapses into molten pockets. Brown butter weaves through every net. There is no shortcut version of this.
