Every LIÈGUER waffle begins 72 hours before it reaches you. The brioche dough — enriched with European butter, slow-developed with time — is cold-pressed to seal in a caramelized crust that shatters on contact.

Pearl sugar pearls imported from Belgium collapse into molten pockets. Brown butter weaves through every net. There is no shortcut version of this.
